Hello, chat

You all know me as projelly (unless you live under a rock, which I no doubt believe a large percentage of WB players do), or just Jelly. As the ending for my love and hate relationship with WB comes closer, I wanted to give back to this game and community. It got me through a lot of troubling and depressing times. The people and this game have given me so much and I'm so very grateful for it. It's safe to say a large part of me grew up through this community. This website is a thank you, a thank you to everyone who supported me, who laughed with me, who fought with me, and to everyone who gave me so much joy. I hope you enjoy it, continue to contribute, make it better.

Help keep the hub useful. This site is meant to grow alongside the community, so corrections, invite links, missing resources, historical notes, and ideas for future hub sections are all helpful. If something is outdated, incomplete, or worth preserving, send it through Discord or GitHub so the index can stay accurate over time.

WB Community Hub is a fan-maintained index for War Brokers resources, community spaces, and game history. The goal is to make scattered community knowledge easier to find, whether someone is looking for tools, Discord servers, tournament spaces, update history, or long-running player-made archives. Contributions, corrections, and suggestions are welcome through Discord or GitHub.
